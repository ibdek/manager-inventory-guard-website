Last updated: 20 September 2026

Manager.io Inventory Guard is an independent software service designed to add inventory, transaction, user-access and integrity controls around supported Manager.io workflows.

1. Service scope

The service may provide controlled Sales Invoice, cash-sale Receipt and Inventory Transfer workflows, stock validation, customer credit controls, sales-price controls, restricted-user Division and Inventory Location controls, automated setup/structure reconciliation, subscription management and integrity monitoring. Available features depend on the linked business configuration.

2. Customer responsibility

You remain responsible for the accuracy of accounting records, inventory configuration, user permissions, customer credit limits, sales prices, access assignments and business decisions made using Manager.io and Inventory Guard.

3. Manager.io access

Inventory Guard requires a valid Manager.io business and, for protected reads and setup operations, an authorised Manager service connection. Inventory Guard does not require the customer's Manager.io login password. Access Tokens and other credentials must be created and managed only by authorised personnel.

4. Trial and subscription

Each new registered business receives a trial allowance of 10 successful controlled transactions. Annual access is charged per registered business for the published 365-day term. Validation failures, refreshes, integrity scans, unsuccessful submissions and duplicate reconciliation of already-counted transactions do not consume trial usage. Trial usage is not reset when annual access is activated.

5. Payments and provisional access

Where supported, payment may be made through Selar or by an approved direct/offline method. A submitted Selar payment reference may receive temporary provisional access while administrator verification is pending. Invalid, duplicate or unverified payment claims may be rejected and provisional access may be withdrawn.

6. Availability and changes

Third-party changes to Manager.io, hosting platforms, browsers or external payment services may affect operation. Reasonable efforts will be made to restore compatibility when supported third-party interfaces change. Product workflows may be updated as controls and automation improve.

7. Acceptable use
